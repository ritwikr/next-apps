Optional

One sign-in, every app

Every app works without an account — your work saves on the device you're using.

  • Learners with a school Google account can sign in once to save their work to their own Google Drive, so it follows them to any device.
  • One sign-in covers all the apps.
  • The apps can only see the files they save there — nothing else in your Drive.
