🕹️ Cheat Codes

🏗️ Build your room

Drag a block from the menu into the room — or tap a block, then tap where it goes. Drag things to move them. Tap ↻ Turn to spin.

✨ Grab a bunch

Hold Shift and tap a few things, or drag a box around them. Now they move together!

↩️ Oops!

Ctrl/⌘ + Z undoes anything. Did it again by mistake? Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + Z redoes.

🪄 Copy magic

Ctrl/⌘ + C to copy, Ctrl/⌘ + V to paste, Ctrl/⌘ + D to make a twin.

🧱 Wall views

You're standing in the middle of the room looking at a wall. You only see the stuff in front of you — and you can only change what you can see!

🪟 Make it grow

Pick a window and tap + on Width or Height. Beams and columns get their own size boxes too.
