Cheat codes

Reading the grid

  • Bass — flat palm, upper-centre of the face. Deep thump.
  • Slap — fingertips flick the top edge and spring off. Sharp crack.
  • Double bass — two quick palm hits in one step.
  • 2Double slap — two quick slaps in one step.
  • RLRight / left hand — used in the rudiments.
  • Rest — play nothing, keep counting.
  • Ghost note — a whisper-soft slap (shown on rests when Ghost notes is on).
  • 1 & 2 &Counts — 1/4 = one step per beat, 1/8 = “1 &”, 1/16 = “1 e & a”. The ringed square is the step sounding now; the dot left of Play flashes on every step.

Practice

Tempo chips
Beats per minute. Start slow — speed comes after accuracy.
Count-in
One bar of 1-2-3-4 clicks (with big numbers) before the groove starts. Remembered between visits.
Ghost notes
Every rest becomes a very soft slap — fills the gaps while the main hits stay loud.
Backing music
A band plays along, locked to your groove.
1 min timer
Stops the groove after one minute of playing — for “hold it without a mistake” drills.
🎤 Timing check
The mic listens while you play and puts a dot under each step: left = early, right = late, centre line = on the beat. The line above sums it up: % of hits within 30 ms, whether you rush or drag, and steadiness (how much your hits wobble — smaller is better). Without 🎧 Headphones the groove and band go quiet and only a soft click plays, so the mic hears just you. Press Calibrate once (speakers: stay quiet; headphones: hit along with the clicks). Results are saved, so you can compare with last time.
⇄ Switch
Alternates between the selected groove and a partner every 4, 2 or 1 bars, on beat 1, without stopping. Tap the small ⇄ on any groove to make it the partner (dashed outline). In the last bar before a change the next groove appears faintly under the grid.
Light delay
Keeps the lit square in step with the sound. Some browsers (Safari especially) play sound a little late; the app corrects what it can by itself (“Auto”). If the square still lights up before you hear the hit, slide right until they match. Remembered on this device.
Volume knobs
Groove = the cajon sounds, Music = the band. Keep the band just under you.
Band styles
Auto picks the band that suits each groove; or choose one yourself.
Match my tempo
Off: your tempo snaps to the band’s own tempo so it sounds natural. On: the band follows your tempo (it gets lower in pitch when slowed down).
Start session
Session = time since you pressed it; Practice = time actually playing.

Songs

Ghost notes
Same switch as in Practice: rests become whisper-soft slaps. Off unless you turn it on (here, in Practice, or with G).
Play song
Plays the whole song start to finish with its band — intro, verses, choruses, breaks and an ending hit.
The sheet
Every section with its groove. One dot per bar; the amber dot is a fill bar (a short burst that leads into the next section). The section playing now is outlined.
Tap a section
Starts the song from there — handy for drilling just the chorus.
Next up
In the last bar of each section the next groove appears faintly under the grid.
Full band
Flannel Static is fully arranged: the band plays its own part in every section — quiet verses, a pre-chorus that builds, big choruses with a melody, real stops, and a final chord. The other songs still use the practice band and just get quieter or louder per section.
