Privacy

Next Apps · Last updated October 2026

In short: your work stays on your device, and — if you choose to sign in — in your own Google Drive. We don't have a database, we don't track you, we don't show ads, and we never sell or share your information.

Who we are

Next Apps are small, free, open-source learning tools (Pixel Pad, Pixel Mosaic and Room Blocks Planner) made by Ritwik Roy with learners at Next School, Mumbai. The code is public on GitHub.

Using the apps without signing in

You don't need an account. Your work is saved in your browser's storage on the device you're using. It isn't sent anywhere.

Signing in with Google (optional)

Signing in lets your work follow you to other devices. It's open only to accounts from approved schools and organisations. When you sign in, we ask Google for:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture — to show who is signed in and to check your account is one that's allowed.
  • Access to files the app creates in your Google Drive — so your work is saved in a "Next Apps" folder in your own Drive. The app can't see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Where your information lives

  • On your device: your work, and your sign-in details (so you stay signed in).
  • In your Google Drive: a copy of your work, if you've signed in. It belongs to you, like any other file in your Drive.
  • Our sign-in helper: a small service run on Cloudflare that handles the steps of signing in with Google and keeps you signed in. It doesn't store anything. The key that keeps you signed in is locked so that only the helper can use it, and it's kept on your device, not by us. Your work never passes through the helper — it goes straight from your device to your Drive.

Like any website, the pages are delivered by GitHub Pages and some apps load typefaces from Google Fonts. These services may record basic technical details such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We never sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We don't use your information or your work to train AI models.
  • We don't read your Drive — only the files the app saved there for you.

Google user data

Next Apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from Google is used only to sign you in and to save and open your own work in your own Drive.

Young learners

These apps are made for school learners. Signing in is only available for school accounts whose school has approved Next Apps, and using the apps without signing in collects nothing.

You're in control

  • Delete any artwork inside the app, or delete the "Next Apps" folder in your Google Drive.
  • Sign out at any time from the account menu.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions? Email roy.ritwik@gmail.com.
