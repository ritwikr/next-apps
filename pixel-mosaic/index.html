🕹️ Cheat Codes

🧱 Draw tiles

Pick a tile from the catalogue, then Draw on the canvas — click, or drag to lay a run. Erase takes tiles away. Hover a swatch to see its name and code.

🪣 Fill an area

The Fill tool floods a whole connected patch of the same tile at once. Great for backgrounds.

💧 Match a tile

Use Pick (or hold Alt/Option while drawing) to grab the tile that's already under your cursor.

▦ Grout & # Grid

Grout is the real joint between tiles (25 mm tiles, 1.97 mm joints — see the size under the width/height). It follows day/night: light grout by day, dark by night. Grid is a faint guide overlay, not in the saved image.

✂️ Select & copy

Drag a box with Select and a little menu pops up — Copy, Mirror, Flip, Delete. Copy, then Paste and tap over the canvas to stamp it anywhere.

🪞 Mirror & reflect

In the selection menu, Mirror ⇆ / Flip ⇅ reflect those tiles in place. While placing a paste they reflect the stamp — perfect for symmetry: copy → paste → mirror → stamp beside the original. (Keys H / V.)

🔍 Move around

Scroll to zoom, Space+drag (or two fingers) to pan. Tap 🔍 or press 0 to snap back to 100%.
